# Code of conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, professional, and harassment-free community.

Be respectful, assume good intent, discuss technical decisions with evidence, protect confidential
information, and accept constructive feedback. Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate
disruption, and publication of private or sensitive information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior conflicts with
these expectations. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Reports will be
handled as confidentially as practical.
